I have read a lot of blogs, tutorials and docs. I never expected to write one.

For a long time I thought blogs belonged to experts, people who had already made it. I changed my mind when I noticed that the posts I learned the most from came from people only a little ahead of me. They remembered what confused them last month, so they explained it well.

## Why I'm writing

I have four reasons:

- To keep notes on what I learn, in my own words.
- To get better at explaining ideas.
- To write on a schedule, even when I don't feel like it.
- To help someone who is as stuck today as I was yesterday.

## What goes here

Most of my time goes to backend development, security, system design and problem solving. Expect posts on all four. Some will be technical notes. Some will be messy. A few will be me thinking out loud about learning.

## Why this post is not perfect

I put this off for weeks because I wanted a perfect first post, with a polished design, complete knowledge and clean writing. None of that showed up, and waiting did not help.

So this is the first post. I will fix the rest as I go.

If you read this far, thanks.
